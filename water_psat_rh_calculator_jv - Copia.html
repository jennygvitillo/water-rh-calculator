IAPWS validity range: 0.01-373.9 °C (273.16-647.10 K). Below 0 °C, dew/frost point input uses the Buck (1996) ice saturation formula, valid to -80 °C, since the IAPWS liquid formula only applies above the triple point.
"RH% @25°C" expresses the absolute water content as the equivalent RH% it would give at 25 °C - useful for comparing isobaric measurements across labs or with literature values, independent of the sample's actual temperature.
